## 1. Positioning

**Positioning statement:** QuoteMint is the simplest quote-to-cash tool for freelancers and micro-studios — create a professional quote, send a link, see the moment the client opens it, and get it accepted online with a typed-name e-signature, for $0 (3 quotes/month) or $9/month flat.

### ICP personas

1. **Solo web/design freelancer** — sends 2–8 quotes/month for $2–20k jobs. Pain: quotes sent as email-attached PDFs disappear; no idea if the client read them; follow-ups are blind. Won by: open tracking + flat $9 pricing.
2. **1–3 person design/marketing studio** — per-seat pricing of incumbents multiplies with every hire. Needs consistent branding (logo text, company name, brand color). Won by: no per-seat pricing + custom branding on Pro.
3. **Independent consultant** (marketing, ops, finance) — credibility matters; output must look professional and print/PDF cleanly. Won by: simplicity and a polished, print-ready quote page.

**Who it is NOT for:** enterprise sales teams needing CPQ/CRM pipelines. Do not dilute positioning to chase them.

(All incumbent figures: team research; verify current pricing pages before quoting publicly.)

**The wedge:**
- **Undercut:** $9/mo flat vs $15–29 entry pricing, and $90/yr (2 months free) for annual.
- **No per-seat:** a 3-person studio pays $9, not $27–87. This is the clearest contrast sentence for landing and outreach.
- **Simplest tool:** one job — create a quote, send a link, track opens, get online acceptance. No pipeline/CRM/templates sprawl to learn.
- **Free plan as distribution:** 3 quotes/month with "Powered by QuoteMint" badge = product-led acquisition; every sent quote exposes a new potential user.
- **Upgrade triggers:** >3 quotes/month, custom branding, view analytics. These map to "I'm making money with this" moments, not vanity features.

### 3.8 Cold outreach for micro-agencies (strategy; templates in OUTREACH.md)

- **Target list sources:** Dribbble/Behance studios, Clutch "small studio" listings, LinkedIn 1–3 person design/marketing agencies, freelancer Slack/Discord communities (verify community promo rules).
- **Volume:** 10 hand-personalized emails/day maximum. 100 generic emails performs worse than 10 real ones and burns the channel.
- **Personalization:** reference a real, specific thing (a portfolio piece, their niche) and tie the pitch to their quoting workflow.
- **One CTA:** try the free plan (3 quotes/month, no card). Never "book a demo".
- **Honesty:** say it's a new product and you're looking for early users whose problems get fixed fast. No fake scarcity.
- **Follow-up:** exactly the day-3/day-7 sequence in OUTREACH.md, then stop. Honor any "stop" immediately.

## 4. First 100 users — 30-day plan

All numbers are hypotheses to validate, not forecasts. One decision gate per week; if a gate fails, fix the funnel before spending launch capital (e.g., delay Product Hunt).

**Week 1 — Foundation + hand outreach**
- Actions: analytics live on the funnel (section 5); Product Hunt "coming soon" page; blog post 1 published; 20 personal outreach emails; 10 helpful community answers.
- Targets (hypotheses): 10 signups; 5 quotes sent; 2 quotes viewed.
- Gate: signup → first quote created ≥ 40%. If below, fix onboarding before any public launch.

**Week 2 — Community-led**
- Actions: posts in r/SideProject + r/SaaS (per rules); Indie Hackers story post; 10 directory submissions; 50 outreach emails cumulative; engage every reply.
- Targets (hypotheses): 35 cumulative signups; 20 cumulative quotes sent; ≥ 25% of sent quotes viewed; 1–2 Pro upgrades; 1 blog post repurposed to X/LinkedIn.
- Gate: at least one channel produces ≥ 5 signups on its own. Double down there in week 4.

**Week 3 — Product Hunt + Show HN week**
- Actions: launch Product Hunt Tue–Thu with full kit (3.1); maker present all day; Show HN same week with 3.3 kit; reply to every comment on both; fix one reported issue publicly and follow up.
- Targets (hypotheses): 75 cumulative signups (assume PH does NOT hit front page; treat any front-page time as upside); 50 cumulative quotes sent; 3–6 cumulative upgrades.
- Gate: launch-week visit → signup ≥ 2%. If below, headline/landing is the problem (test candidates in section 7).

**Week 4 — Double down + retention check**
- Actions: 2x effort on the best-performing channel from week 2; blog posts 2 and 3 published; follow-up sequence run to completion on all outreach; short "you asked, we fixed it" update post.
- Targets (hypotheses): 100 cumulative signups; 70 cumulative quotes sent; 5–8 cumulative upgrades; ≥ 30% of week-1 signups still active (created a quote in the last 7 days).
- Gate: quote sent → viewed ≥ 50%. If below, client-side experience needs work (subject line, spam placement, link clarity).

## 5. Funnel metrics to watch

| Stage | Metric | Hypothesis target | If below target, fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visit → signup | Landing conversion | ≥ 3% | Headline clarity; test section 7 candidates; simplify hero to one CTA |
| Signup → first quote | Activation | ≥ 40% | Empty state; pre-filled example template; 60-second onboarding path |
| First quote → sent | Send rate | ≥ 60% | Share-step friction (copy link, email delivery); make the "send" action unmissable |
| Sent → viewed | Client open rate | ≥ 50% | Email deliverability of notifications/reminders; resend + reminder features; link previews |
| Viewed → accepted | Acceptance | ≥ 25% | Quote quality help; follow-up prompt after N views; make the accept button unmissable |
| Accepted → upgrade | Free-to-Pro | 5–8% | Paywall timing (after value, not before); message analytics + branding as upsell; revisit at 4th quote attempt |

Also track weekly (one number each, no dashboards): quotes sent per active user, time-to-first-quote from signup, 7-day return rate. These reveal whether the core loop repeats, which no single funnel stage shows.
