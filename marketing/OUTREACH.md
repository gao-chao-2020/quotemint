# QuoteMint — Outreach Templates

> [中文](OUTREACH.zh-CN.md) | English

Cold outreach rules (apply to every template below):
- 10 hand-personalized messages per day maximum. Volume without personalization burns the channel and the brand.
- Every template has exactly one CTA: try the free plan (3 quotes/month, no card needed).
- Never invent statistics, testimonials, or user numbers. "New product, looking for early users" is the honest and effective framing.
- Subject lines: short, lowercase, plain, specific. No "Quick question?" clickbait.
- Plain text only. No attachments, no images, no tracking pixels beyond normal link UTM tags.
- Any "stop" / "no thanks" ends the sequence immediately. Never re-add someone.
- Append UTM tags to links so replies can be measured: ?utm_source=outreach&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=week1 (adjust per wave).
- Track per wave: emails sent, replies, signups attributed, quotes created by those signups. Two numbers per wave is enough.

## 1. Cold email — freelance individuals (web/design/marketing)

Target: solo freelancers found via portfolios, communities, or referrals.
Subject: how do you know when a quote gets opened

> Hi [Name],
>
> I saw your [specific work/project] — the [specific detail] stood out.
>
> I'm building QuoteMint because of a problem I kept hitting: you send a quote and then just wait, with no idea if the client even opened it. QuoteMint turns a quote into a link that shows when it's opened, and clients can accept it online with a typed-name signature. Prints to PDF cleanly.
>
> The free plan does 3 quotes a month, no card needed.
>
> Would you try one real quote through it? If it's not useful, just say so and I won't email again.
>
> [Name], founder, QuoteMint
> [link]

(99 words. Personalization line is mandatory — replace both brackets with something real.)

## 5. Follow-up sequence (after template 1, 2, or 3; same email thread)

Send day 3 and day 7. Then stop permanently, whether or not there was a reply.

**Day 3 — one short bump, same thread, reply to your own email:**

> Quick bump — did this land? If quoting tools aren't a priority right now, no problem at all. One thing worth knowing if you do try it: the "opened" notification fires the moment the client clicks your quote link, so your first follow-up can wait until they've actually read it. [link]

**Day 7 — final note, same thread:**

> Last note from me. If you ever want to know the moment a client opens your quote, QuoteMint's free plan does 3 quotes a month: [link]. If not, all good — I won't email again. [Name]

**Rules for the sequence:**
- Never send the next step if the person replied with anything, even "not now" — answer them like a human instead.
- Never send more than these two follow-ups. A third is spam.
- A wave is done after day 7; log the numbers (section header) and move to the next target list.
